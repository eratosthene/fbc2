How to write it

  • It is HTML. eBay doesn't allow scripts or forms, or links to other sites except images and videos. Plain tags such as <p>, <h3> and <ul> are the safe choice.
  • {value} is replaced by the Unit's value, with anything special escaped. It is blank when the Unit has none.
  • {if name}...{end} keeps what is between only when the Unit has that value. {if a or b} keeps it when either has one.
  • Line breaks and the spaces at the start of a line are ignored.
