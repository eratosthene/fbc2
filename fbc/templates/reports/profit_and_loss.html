Cost of goods answers "were the things sold this period sold at a profit?": buying a big Lot in December doesn't make the year look like a loss. Cash answers "did the shop make or spend money this period?". Over all time, cash profit is the home page's total net profit. A Lot's price is split evenly over its Units, so a Lot's cost in one month is an average.
