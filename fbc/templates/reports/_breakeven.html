Break-even per Unit is the least an average Unit can sell for to cover its cost and its supplies, after fees: cost per Unit ÷ (1 − fee %) + supplies per Unit. All time is the home page's figure. The period's uses the cost of the Units sold in it, so a short period can swing; all time is the steadier one.
