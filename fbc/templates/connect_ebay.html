Connect eBay

fbc signs in to eBay with OAuth for everything it does there: syncing listings and orders, eBay Drafts and Ship This. It needs your permission once, keeps the token eBay gives it (good for about 18 months) and renews its access from that. If you connected before fbc stopped using a user token from config.py, connect again so syncing and listing keep working.
